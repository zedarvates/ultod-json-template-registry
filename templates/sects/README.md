# Sects templates

## Purpose

Minimal declarative sects identity and classification.

## Required fields

`display_name`. Optional kind, category, domain, alignment, school, and rarity tags are defined by the exact schema.

## Authoritative exclusions

Excluded: stats, costs, cooldowns, awareness, AI behavior, dialogue, schedules, loot, shops, permissions, standings, powers, blessings, curses, effects, lore, and asset paths.

## Intended consumers

LLM pipeline only. Zig support requires separate typed-adapter evidence.

## Compatibility evidence

None verified.

## Versioning

Templates and family schemas follow independent SemVer.
