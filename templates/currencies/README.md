# Currencies templates

## Purpose

Minimal declarative currencies identity and classification.

## Fields

`display_name` and `currency_code`.

## Authoritative exclusions

Excluded: progression, rewards, stats, timings, probabilities, spawn rules, balances, lore, dialogue, and asset paths.

## Intended consumers

LLM pipeline only. Zig support requires separate typed-adapter evidence.

## Compatibility evidence

None verified.

## Versioning

Templates and family schemas follow independent SemVer.
